Current design

Castellammarese-American Artwork

The current flag and Castle & Eagle emblem are the primary artwork for new merchandise, printing, patches, and other applications.

Castellammarese-American Flag

Nine alternating red-and-yellow stripes, beginning and ending with red, with the Castle & Eagle of Castellammare del Golfo in the blue canton.

Download SVG
The current flag uses the original approved layout with the updated Castle & Eagle emblem. Production color standard: Pantone 2945 C blue, Pantone 1795 C red, Pantone 116 C yellow/gold, and Pantone Black C for outlines.

Castle & Eagle

The red tower, gold eagle, and gold cross as a standalone emblem for patches, pins, shirts, hats, and other merchandise.
